Flower Care

Keeping your flowers beautiful is simple.

How do I remove dust? expand_more

Use a soft brush to dust gently. If using a hair dryer, choose cool air and a low setting.

How do I reshape a flower? expand_more

Gently bend the flexible stem and open or close the petals to suit your arrangement.

How do I protect flowers from water and sunlight? expand_more

Keep these flowers away from water and prolonged direct sunlight.
